# 🪙 CAS COIN CAN NOW BE PURCHASED ON OPENSEA

We are pleased to announce that **CAS (Cryptocoin Agentic Space)** can now be acquired through **OpenSea**, expanding once again the official channels for accessing the Agentic Space ecosystem's coin.

## What is OpenSea?

OpenSea is one of the largest and best-known decentralized marketplaces for digital assets in the world. Founded in 2017, the platform became famous for NFT (non-fungible token) trading and has evolved to also support buying and selling fungible tokens (ERC-20) across multiple networks — including **Polygon PoS**, where CAS was issued.

In practice, OpenSea works as a showcase and a trading interface on top of the blockchain: you connect your wallet (such as MetaMask, Rabby or Coinbase Wallet), pick the asset you want and the platform routes the swap through the best available liquidity sources — including DEX pools like Uniswap — without you having to interact directly with the contracts.

## How to buy CAS on OpenSea

1. Go to the official token page: **[CAS on OpenSea](https://opensea.io/token/polygon/0x5151a34eac7ba08cd6b540b32cd30316218a2287)**
2. Connect your wallet on the **Polygon network (chainId 137)**.
3. Choose the payment asset (POL, USDC or another supported one) and confirm the swap in your wallet.

⚠️ **Warning:** always verify the official CAS contract address before confirming any transaction:

`0x5151A34EaC7bA08cd6B540b32cD30316218A2287`

## What does this change?

- **One more official channel** to acquire CAS, alongside the native CASSwap in Agentic Space and the Uniswap pools;
- **More visibility**: being on OpenSea makes it easier for new participants to discover and acquire the coin through a familiar interface;
- **Same on-chain security**: liquidity still comes from the official pools — OpenSea acts as an access layer.

CASSwap remains the native and recommended way to interact with the ecosystem; OpenSea becomes a complementary alternative, especially for those who already use the platform.
